# Nashama JO

### Reducing Food Waste Through Community Food Sharing

Nashama JO is a mobile application concept designed to reduce food waste in Jordan by connecting food donors with charities and individuals who need food. The platform aims to make surplus food redistribution more accessible, organized, and dignified.

## 🌱 The Problem

Large quantities of edible food are discarded while individuals and families face food insecurity. Nashama JO aims to bridge this gap by facilitating the redistribution of surplus food to people who can benefit from it.

## 💡 Key Features

- **User Roles:** Support for food donors, charitable organizations, and beneficiaries.
- **Food Donations:** Add and browse available surplus food donations.
- **Food Reservations:** Enable beneficiaries to reserve available food.
- **Donation Management:** Support donation cancellation, pickup confirmation, and donation history.
- **Pickup Verification:** Pickup codes to help coordinate food collection.
- **Expiry Awareness:** Food expiry information and reminder-related functionality.
- **Account Management:** Registration, login, OTP-related screens, and profile settings.
- **Role-Based Interfaces:** Different home screens and workflows for donors, charities, and beneficiaries.
- **Arabic-Friendly Interface:** Designed with support for Arabic and right-to-left layouts.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Flutter**
- **Dart**
- **Provider** for state management
- **Firebase-related dependencies and configuration** — exact service integrations are to be confirmed against the final source code.
